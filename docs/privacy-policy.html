2. What data FLOW collects

FLOW is a calorie and macro-nutrient tracking app. Using it means giving us personal data, which we collect and store in a securely hosted database (see Section 5) in order to run your account and the features below:

Account data. Email address and password, used only for authentication via our backend provider, Supabase (see Section 5). We never see or store your password in plain text; it is handled entirely by Supabase's authentication system.

Profile data, entered by you in the app: name, birth year, gender, height, current weight, fitness goal (lose, maintain, or gain weight), activity level, and your personal targets for water, protein, and fat intake.

Nutrition and activity data: - Food log entries: food name, portion size, and calories/macros, associated with a meal type (breakfast, lunch, dinner, or snack) and a date. - Saved "meals" you create (a named group of ingredients you can quickly re-add). - Exercise log entries (calories burned, date). - Water intake log entries (amount, date). - Weight history entries (weight, date, optional notes).

Usage data needed to run the app. Which one-time or account-wide feature limits you've used (for example, whether you've already created your one free saved meal, or shared a QR code), and which optional paid add-ons are active on your account. This is limited to counters and on/off flags; it does not include general behavioral analytics (see "What we do not collect" below). We also track whether you've completed the in-app tour, so it doesn't repeat unnecessarily.

Device-local preferences that are not sent to our servers: your chosen theme, language, and unit system (metric or imperial), and your meal-reminder notification schedule. These stay on your device only.

Camera. FLOW asks for camera access to scan barcodes (to look up a food's nutrition info) and to scan QR codes (to import a meal or a day's log that someone else shared with you). The camera feed is processed on your device in real time to read the barcode or QR code. FLOW does not save photos or camera footage, and does not upload images anywhere.

Advertising. FLOW shows ads to free-tier users through Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and ad-interaction data, and processes it under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). If you're in the EEA, UK, or another region with similar consent requirements, we'll ask for your consent to personalized advertising through Google's consent management tool before showing personalized ads, and you can change that choice at any time from Settings. Purchasing the "Remove Ads" add-on stops ads from being shown on your account (see Section 8).

What we do not collect. Beyond the advertising data described above, we do not run any separate analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDK, so we have no visibility into your day-to-day usage patterns beyond the counters described earlier. Push notifications are also scheduled entirely on your device, using a local-notification library rather than a remote push service, so no notification data is sent to or through our servers.

3. Why we collect it and what we use it for

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign in.
  • To calculate your personalized calorie and macro targets, and to show you your logs, history, and progress.
  • To enforce the free-tier limits and paid add-ons described in the Terms of Service, for example checking whether a feature is unlocked for your account.
  • To show ads to free-tier users, and to let you remove them via the "Remove Ads" add-on.
  • To send you the meal-reminder notifications you configure, handled locally on your device.
  • To respond if you contact us for support.

We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

4. Sharing a log or meal via QR code

When you use the "Share" feature, FLOW encodes the relevant data (for example, a day's food log or a saved meal) directly into a QR code image on your device. That QR code is not uploaded to our servers; it only contains the data itself, embedded in the image, and is shared however you choose to share the image (AirDrop, Messages, saving it to your photo library, and so on). Anyone who scans that QR code with FLOW can import the data it contains, so be mindful of who you share it with, since the QR code itself contains your logged food data.

5. Third parties we share data with

We keep the list of third parties who can access your data as short as possible:

Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider, hosts our database and handles authentication. Supabase stores your account and app data on our behalf, acting as our data processor. Supabase's own privacy and security practices are described at supabase.com/privacy.

Google AdMob shows ads to free-tier users and, in doing so, may collect the device and advertising data described in Section 2. Google's privacy practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy.

Open Food Facts, a free, open, non-profit food database, receives your search term or barcode number directly whenever you search for a food by name or scan a barcode, in order to look up nutrition information. This request is not linked to your FLOW account or email; it's a one-off lookup, the same as if you searched their website directly. Their privacy practices are described at world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Payment processor (once in-app purchases go live). As explained in Section 8 and in the Terms of Service, FLOW's paid add-ons do not charge real money yet. Once they do, purchases will be processed through Google Play Billing (Android) and, if FLOW launches on iOS, Apple's In-App Purchase system - we will not see, receive, or store your payment card details ourselves; these are handled entirely by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies (policies.google.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy).

We do not otherwise share, rent, or sell your personal data to third parties. If that ever changes, we will update this Policy first and, where required by law, ask for your consent.

6. How long we keep your data

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account from within the app:

  • Your account is scheduled for deletion and enters a 30-day grace period.
  • If you sign back in during those 30 days, the deletion is automatically cancelled and your account is restored as-is.
  • After 30 days, your account and all associated data (food logs, weight history, saved meals, exercise logs, and profile information) are permanently and irreversibly deleted.

If you'd rather not wait, the deletion screen also offers a "No, delete my data now" option that skips the 30-day grace period entirely: choosing it permanently deletes your account and all associated data immediately. This cannot be undone, even by signing back in.

You can also ask us any question about data we hold on you, or request deletion by email instead of through the app, by emailing faycal.ghozzi@gmail.com.

7. Your rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with similar data protection laws, you generally have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (most profile and log data can be edited directly in the app).
  • Delete your data (see Section 6, or contact us for anything the in-app deletion doesn't cover).
  • Export your data in a portable format. FLOW's Progress screen lets you export your logs as a PDF at any time; contact us if you need a raw data export instead.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, including advertising consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In France, this is the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés, www.cnil.fr).

To exercise any of these rights, email faycal.ghozzi@gmail.com. We will respond within one month, as required by GDPR.

9. Children's privacy

If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without appropriate consent, please contact us at faycal.ghozzi@gmail.com so we can delete it.

10. Data security

We rely on Supabase's infrastructure, hosted in the EU (Ireland), which encrypts data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest, and enforces row-level security so that your data is only accessible to your own authenticated account. Any file storage FLOW uses is kept private, not publicly accessible. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your data given the scale of this project.

11. International data transfers

Your core account and app data (Section 2) lives in a dedicated database hosted on Supabase's infrastructure in the EU (Ireland), within the European Economic Area. Some of Supabase's supporting infrastructure (for example, system logs and real-time features) runs on sub-processors located outside the EEA, including in the United States. Where this results in a transfer of personal data outside the EEA, Supabase relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and, for several of its sub-processors, their own certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as the lawful basis for that transfer, documented in Supabase's Data Processing Agreement and Transfer Impact Assessment.

Google AdMob, which shows ads to free-tier users, is a global service and may process the advertising-related data described in Section 2/5 outside the EEA. Where this occurs, Google relies on its own safeguards, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and its own EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework certification, for lawful international transfer of personal data. The same will apply to Google Play Billing or Apple's In-App Purchase system once real billing is enabled (Section 5).
